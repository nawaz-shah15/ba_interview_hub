### How to move a company

Open `src/data/industryOverrides.json` and add the company id and the industry (`bfsi`, `healthcare` or `others`). Example:

```json
{
  "allegis-solutions": "bfsi",
  "ey-l1": "healthcare"
}
```

The company ids are in `src/data/questions.json` (the `id` field of each company). Save the file and the app moves the company straight away.
